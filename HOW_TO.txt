TRUSTLENS POPUP - HOW TO USE (Windows, Chrome)

First: right-click trustlens-popup.zip > Extract All. Keep all files together in the folder.

A) DEMO INBOX (no install, use this for your demo)
1. Double-click inbox.html. It opens in your browser.
2. Click any message. A TrustLens popup appears at the top right.
3. Click "Close" (or press Esc) to dismiss it.

B) GMAIL EXTENSION (warns on your real Gmail)
1. Open Chrome and type  chrome://extensions  in the address bar. Press Enter.
2. Turn ON "Developer mode" (switch at the top right).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the extracted trustlens-popup folder.
4. Open (or refresh) mail.google.com.
5. Open any email. If it looks risky, the popup appears. Safe emails show nothing.

TO TEST IT SAFELY
From another account, email yourself this invented text, then open it
(check Spam if it is not in your inbox):
  Subject: Account blocked
  Your SBI account is blocked immediately. Click http://sbi-kyc-update.xyz to update your KYC and enter your OTP

IF YOU CHANGE ANY FILE: go to chrome://extensions, click the refresh arrow on TrustLens, then refresh Gmail.

PRIVACY: everything runs on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere. The popup never clicks, replies, or deletes anything.

LIMITS: Gmail changes its page layout now and then, so the extension may need a small fix later.
SMS on a real phone needs a mobile app. The demo inbox shows the same warning for SMS.
